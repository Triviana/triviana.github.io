Privacy Policy

Triviana · Last updated 17 August 2026

Triviana is a pass-and-play trivia game. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that identifies you personally.

The short version. We collect anonymous gameplay statistics so we can tell which questions are too easy or too hard. Nothing we collect identifies you. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, and photos you take in the app never leave your device.

What we collect

When analytics is enabled (it is on by default and can be switched off at any time), the app records anonymous gameplay events and sends them to our own server:

  • A random device identifier. The app generates this itself from a timestamp and a random number the first time it runs. It is not your device's serial number, advertising identifier, or any hardware ID, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • A session identifier — a random value that groups events from one sitting.
  • Gameplay events — such as a game starting, a question being shown, whether a question was answered correctly, how long the answer took, and a game finishing. These are about the questions, not about you.
  • An optional age bracket. The app asks once whether you are Under 18, 18–24, 25–30, or 31+. This is entirely optional — you can skip it, and it is stored only as one of those four broad ranges. There is deliberately no under-13 option.
  • A timestamp for each event.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses.
  • No accounts or passwords — the app has none.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, calendars, or files.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. We do not use ad networks, and we do not share anything with data brokers.
  • No photos. If you use the camera or photo library to set a team avatar, that image is stored on your device only. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Why we collect it

For one purpose: tuning question difficulty. Knowing that a question is answered correctly 95% of the time tells us it is priced too easy. That is the whole reason this data exists. We do not profile you, sell data, or use it for advertising.

Turning it off

Open Settings in the app and switch analytics off. From that moment nothing further is recorded, and any events still waiting to be sent are discarded rather than uploaded.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts our database and receives the anonymous events described above.
  • Apple processes any purchase you make. We never see or store your payment details.
  • RevenueCat tells the app whether a purchase is active. It does not receive your gameplay data.
  • Media providers. Some questions load images or audio from outside services. As with loading any web page, those services can see your device's IP address as part of the request. We do not send them any other information about you.

How long we keep it

Anonymous event data is kept only as long as it is useful for tuning questions, and is pruned periodically. Because the data is anonymous and there are no accounts, we have no way to link it back to a person.

Children

Triviana is not directed at children under 13, and the optional age question has no under-13 option. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can switch analytics off in Settings at any time, and uninstalling the app removes the random device identifier along with everything else stored locally. If you have a question about this policy, contact us using the details on our support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
