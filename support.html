Common questions

I paid for the full version but it is still locked.
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. That re-checks your purchase with the App Store. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If it still does not unlock, email us.
I want a refund.
All purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us. Use Apple's Report a Problem tool or contact Apple Support directly. See our Terms & Conditions for details.
Do I have to pay again on my other device?
No. The unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. Install the app on the other device, then tap Restore purchase.
Does the game work offline?
Yes, once it has loaded its questions the first time. Some questions use images or audio, and those need a connection.
A question looks wrong, or the answer is incorrect.
Please tell us — send the category and the question wording and we will fix it. Questions are updated from our server, so corrections reach you without an app update.
How do I turn off analytics?
Open Settings and switch analytics off. See our privacy policy for exactly what is collected and why.
A category says "Coming soon".
That category has no questions in it yet. New content is added regularly and arrives automatically — no app update needed.
How do I delete my data?
The app has no accounts and stores nothing that identifies you. Uninstalling removes everything the app kept on your device, including team avatars and the random identifier used for anonymous statistics.
