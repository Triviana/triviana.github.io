Triviana

A pass-and-play trivia game for the room. English and Arabic.

Pick your categories, split into teams, and pass the phone around. Thousands of questions across dozens of categories — from football and films to anime, chess, and world capitals.

How it plays

  • Two to six teams on a single device — no accounts, no sign-up.
  • Choose six categories; each has five point tiers from 100 to 500.
  • Miss a question and the next team can steal it.
  • Fully bilingual — every question is written in both English and Arabic.
  • Plays offline once loaded.
