2. The App

Triviana is a bilingual (English/Arabic), pass-and-play trivia game designed to be played by a group sharing a single device. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login. Some categories are free; the remaining categories can be unlocked with a single optional purchase (see §7). Question content is served from our server and updates from time to time without requiring an app update; the App is also designed to keep working offline once its question bank has been downloaded, except for individual questions that use images or audio, which require an internet connection to load.
